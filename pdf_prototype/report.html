Limits

  • The rewrite has not received a full scientific fact check.
  • OCR can misread numbers, formulas, figures, or tables even when section labels are correct.
  • Generated tables are hidden in the reading view. Consult the original PDF, not OCR or rewritten cells, for authoritative numbers.
  • Table fragments remain separate across page breaks. Figures are available on the original pages; no figure-understanding step was tested.
  • Confidence thresholds are provisional. Uncertain blocks were inspected before rewriting; this was not a fully unattended success.
  • This prototype accepts English research PDFs up to 20 pages / 25 MiB. It refuses incomplete OCR, missing required sections, and unresolved labels rather than silently proceeding.
  • Extracted text went to TypeSafe. OCR and rewriting ran on the local server; no public queue or deployment was changed.
